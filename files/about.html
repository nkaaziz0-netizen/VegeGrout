About Us

An academic-industry team, ten years into one problem.

Vege-Grout began as university research into bio-mediated soil improvement and has grown into a commercially deployed, patented system — built on a continuing partnership between UNITEN's research team and industry.

RC
Project Lead · UNITEN

Prof. Madya Dr. Rohayu Che Omar

Leads the research team behind Vege-Grout's formulation, from the original 2009–2011 lab findings through to the current field-proven system and its granted patent.

GT
Industry Collaborator

Geomapping Technology Sdn. Bhd.

Commercial and technical partner handling bulk production, geotechnical design, and skilled installation crews for current deep-seated landslide projects.

Ten Years of R&D

From lab formulation to field-proven system.

2009 – 2011

Formulation research

Promising early findings on fermented vegetable-waste formulation, dosage ratios, and curing time.

2013

Patent filed

"Soil and Rock Remediation Process and a Remedial Composition Modification using Vegetable Waste" — Application PI 2013701380.

2013 – 2015

Lab-scale proof of concept

Trials at UNITEN's own student parking lot and at KM22.2, Sg. Betis (Gua Musang–Simpang Pulai).

2016

Pilot-scale validation

Production enhancements and scale-up across multiple soil types, topographies, and slope conditions.

2017 – 2020

Market traction

First paid commercial projects with OPUS Berhad, MF Construction, and Landasan Kapital Sdn. Bhd.

2020

Patent granted

Grant Number MY-174566-A — the system moves from filed to fully protected IP.

Present

TRL 7 — deep-seated projects underway

Active deployment on deep-seated landslide sites in partnership with Geomapping Technology Sdn. Bhd.
