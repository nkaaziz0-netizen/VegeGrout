The 5-stage SOP

Coverage spreads the way the grout itself does.

01

Site Clearing

Trim the slope to a stable, uniform profile and clear loose debris before survey work begins.

02

Resistivity Survey

Map groundwater and subsurface layers to pinpoint exact injection points.

03

Grout Injection

Vege-Grout is injected via PVC piping and surface-sprayed at 3.7 L/m² across the grid.

04

COLUFNAS & Seeding

Coir matting and hydroseeding lock in erosion control while roots take hold.

05

Lab Verification

Samples tested to BS 1377 confirm strength, permeability, and stability gains.
